Buttered Noodles

Ingredients

  • Noodles
  • Butter
  • Salt
  • Parmesan

Instructions

  1. Boil water and salt it until it tastes like the ocean.
  2. Cook the pasta until it's ready (taste test every few minutes).
  3. Mix in butter and salt.
  4. Top with a bunch of parm.
